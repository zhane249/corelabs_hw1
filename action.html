True Education Summary

This manhua is about a teacher that had lost his fiancee and was recruited by the fiancee's father who was the head of the Education Rights Protection Beureu, right under the Ministry of Education. The fiancee was a teacher who had lost her life to a student after repeatedly trying to help he get on the right track. This was the final straw to a continuous ongoing pattern where becuase of the absence of corporal punishment as well as the children under 14 years old could not be sentenced no matter what severe crime they commit. The teachers hesitant to speak out and educate as well as discipline, and students grew unrewly. Parents pressure schools with unresonable complaints, and principles and school attendants were too busyy covering up the data. Thus began the series of endevours Na Hwa-jin and his team goes on to investigate the truth behind every one of the cases they receive, whether it is students being bullied or teachers, and execute the rightful punishment despite the controversy of being vigilantes.

How to Fight Summary

This manhwa revolves around Hobin Yu, a highschool student that was targeted by bullies. By chance he stumbles across a NewTube video that provided practical advice on street fights, thus he began filming his encounters. After a confrontation went viral, he began using knowledge from the internet to become a fighter, encountering rivals as well as friends. Note that there are mentions of Hobin Yu in Juvenile Offender, where he acts as temporary mentor to the main character there, and so does one of his friends. The art style is very clear and detailed, and the story is well written with turning points and a fulfilling character arch.

Juvenile Offender Summary

This manhwa has very similar art style to How to Fight as well as True Education, and serves as a potential sequal to How to Fight. The main character is Yunseong Lee, a student who suffered severe bullying by a group of students at school, and later dropped out due to the bullying, as well as damage to his family. He decided to give up on his life in order to obtain revenge against the bullies, who have grown and didn't suffer any consequences. His journey takes him to prison, to another high school, boats, and many different locations. Through his journey, he sinks further into the quicksand. He gathered many likeminded people to help with his revenge, and ultimately results in a tragic end, but perhaps happiness and peace, depends on who is looking at it.
