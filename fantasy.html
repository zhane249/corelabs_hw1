Eternally Regressing Knight

The story follows Encrid, a knight that after dying on the battlefield, is stuck in a loop of life and death, repeating endless "today"s until he manages to survive. Each time he dies, he comes face to face with "death" and he asks if he wants to continue, yet Encrid never breaks, as he thinks of this curse as a blessing that would allow him, a warrior with no talent, to be able to progress. He leads a group of so-called lunatics, that no other leader was able to control. After his curse started, Encrid was able to learn from his subcordinates the necessary skills to overcome each of his walls and progress to the next day. In doing so, he inspired many others who remained stagnant just surviving day after day to strive for more.

On the Way to Meet Mom

The beautiful illustration follows Mori, a abandoned boy that lived in the wild until he was found by the rescue team and placed into a children's center for humans. The world is now run by aliens, and humans are under the protection of them. However, because of the history of distrust, and the suspicion of the kindness granted by the aliens, half of the humans chose to survive on their own independance, whereas a few others decided to come under the Aliens' wing, and to live in "paradise". Mori goes through physical and emotional hurdals as he meets his "mother", Ryder and then later on his other mother. The story goes to show that family is something you find, it doesn't necessarily has to be predetermined.

The Earth's Chosen Savior

This particular read was quite unique, in the sense that the "system" fighting against the apololypse is designed by Earth to save itself from the incoming invasion by apien species. The quiz took form in short questions that determined the category of human, predator or savior. The standards however, are unlike what usually comes associated with those terms. Jeong-u Park is the main character, and one of the few saviors chosen. His goal is to close the portals using points earned through gathering essence left by living creatures, and to compete life or death game with other saviors around the country and world to gain privelages.
