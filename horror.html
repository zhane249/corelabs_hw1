Campfire Friends

The tired office worker Chae-Soo dreamt about his favorite childhood TV show, Campfire Friends! After a long tiring day at the office and unpleasant interactions with his child and wife. After scanning the QR code printed, he was transported to the world of the TV show whenever he falls asleep. He rotates between world, growing ever more distant to reality as in the dream designed so theres nothing he dislike, he is able to thrive. However, things take a dark turn as Chae-Soo discovers slowly the dark secrets that the Campfire Friends are hiding, and soon he finds that perhaps this world isn't fictional after all.

Memorial

This webtoon is set in a dystopian modern world where because of the deadly gas that is passed around, the government developed Memorial Chips that would be able to safeguard people from the gas, or so they say. However, there are times when these chips malfunction, and that is where the the animal bots come in. Stationed throughout every city, consistently roaming around, the animal bots would be able to "fix" the chip. And if there is any damage to the chip implanted into the neck, there would be a so-called malfunction. The main character, Minhui, a highschool student discovers one day that the girl he liked suddenly disappeared, and no one remembered her but him. Thus he starts to uncover the surveiliance that is consisentently around, and the danger of reality.

Momfluencer

The art style and story outline is ever so unique. Following Chaerin's Mom, Juyeon Park, the webtoon unfolds of the struggling beginnings of a momfluencer, and comparison of the main character's life to her highschool "friend", Yehui Kim, a social media influencer. We follow as Juyeon Park slowly reveals herself to be an unreliable narrator, and seeing the same events happening from other character's perspectives. It really sheds light on the two sided nature of humans, and the depth of morals.
